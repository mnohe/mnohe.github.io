Braid is a tool-independent methodology for coordinating multiple actors around a software product, whether they are humans, coding agents, or a mix of both. It makes project intent, ownership, dependencies, and independent verification explicit so work can proceed safely across concurrent and short-lived sessions. Discoveries and failed attempts remain with the project as durable memory, while a defined workflow keeps the next step predictable without requiring a dedicated project-management platform.
