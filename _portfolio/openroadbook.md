OpenRoadBook is an open, human-friendly, machine-readable format for roadbooks,
designed for adventure, overland, and non-competitive events with clear
validation and export tooling. The project provides a canonical YAML/JSON
structure, a symbol registry of open SVG assets, validators and CLI tooling,
print/render templates, and converters for GPX/KML and printable roadbooks.
