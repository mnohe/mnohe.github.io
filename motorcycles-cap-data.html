The three indices from The two-wheeled unicorn applied to a selection of bikes, including all those named in the article. Green cells satisfy the relevant index (≥ 1); amber cells do not. Affordability is calculated against local GDP per capita at the time of pricing. Hover any index value to see the calculation; hover the feature count for the comfort breakdown. Lean angle is the maximum before the bike touches down.
